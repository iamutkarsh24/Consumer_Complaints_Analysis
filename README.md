# Consumer Complaints Data Analysis!
![Consumer Complaints Data Analysis](https://lawtrend.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/consumer-complaint.jpeg)

## About the Project 🚀
The project involves analyzing a dataset with 65,500 rows and 18 columns related to consumer complaints. The goal 
is to gain meaningful insights into customer grievances to improve products, services, and overall customer satisfaction, including:

- **Complaints Received**: All the complaints received by the company from the customers.

- **Complaints Resolved**: All the complaints resolved by the company within the given timeframe.

- **Issue**: Type of problem people or customers are facing while using the products or the services.

- **Product**: Type of products customers are using of the company.

- **Key Metrics**: Evaluation of critical metrics such as time taken to solve the issue, product and sub-products, and customer relationship and optimize strategies.

## Problem Statement ❓
- Find out how many complaints are sent and received on the same day. 
- Extract the complaints received in the state of New York.
- Extract the complaints received in the states of New York and California.
- Extract all the rows with the word 'credit' in the products field.
- Extract all the rows with the word 'Late' in the issue field.
  
## Objectives of the Project 🎯
- **Identify the common issue**: Pinpoint frequent problems reported by consumers to address recurring issues.
- **Improve customer satisfaction**: Enhance product or service quality based on feedback.
- **Enhance brand reputation**: Build trust by showing customers that their concerns are taken seriously.
- **Comply with Regulations**: Ensure the company adheres to regulatory standards for consumer rights and protection.
